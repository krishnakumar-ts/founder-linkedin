CASE 01

11 months as the LinkedIn voice of a founder-CEO in the AWS partner ecosystem

ROLE
Ghostwriter and content planner
FORMATS
Text posts, carousels, event campaigns, gated lead-gen posts

Problem

A founder-CEO with ~24K followers and deep AWS ecosystem knowledge, and no time to write. Every post had to work for buyers, partners and the AWS field at once.

Approach

Write from the founder's own opinions, not company news, and plan the calendar around the AWS event year, when buyers and AWS sellers pay the most attention.

Execution

Wrote 50+ posts in the founder's voice. Ran campaigns around AWS re:Invent, AWS Summits and the Marketplace Seller Conference. Built gated posts where readers comment to get a resource, which opens a conversation.

Outcome

Eleven months of steady founder posting, every post approved by the founder. Client name and account numbers stay private.
